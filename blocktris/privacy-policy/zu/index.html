Stack Rush / 스택 러시

Le nqubomgomo yobumfihlo ichaza indlela i-Stack Rush ephatha ngayo ulwazi lohlelo lwe-Android olushicilelwe ngaphansi kwegama lephakheji com.makeitbetter.stackrush.

Usuku lokuqala ukusebenza: 2026-10-24

Ukususa idatha yebhodi lezikhundla · Ukwesekwa nolwazi lomshicileli

Isaziso sokusebenza kwezici ongazikhetha: ukubuyekezwa kwangomhla ka-22 ku-Okthoba 2026 kulungiselela izikhangiso zomvuzo ze-Android ukuze zisetshenziswe ngaphakathi komkhawulo obuyekeziwe ovikela izingane, ongenzi izikhangiso ngokomuntu, nangokuvumelana nokukhetha okusebenza kumdlali. Ukutholakala kuncike enguqulweni efakiwe, ukufaneleka kwesicelo ku-Google nasekutholakaleni kwezikhangiso. Ukucubungula okusha kwebhodi lezikhundla nokukhangisa kwe-Apps in Toss kusamisiwe ukuze kuhlolwe ukusebenza ngokwehlukile. Ukudlala okuyisisekelo kuqala ngaphandle kokwamukela ubumfihlo konke ndawonye, futhi ukushintsha ukukhetha kwezikhangiso akuvali umdlalo. Ukubuyekeza leli khasi akubuyekezi izinhlelo ezifakiwe futhi akuqinisekisi ukuvela kwezikhangiso noma imivuzo. Incazelo ihlanganisa nenguqulo ezindala nezicelo zamalungelo eziphathelene namarekhodi akhona.

1. Okwenziwa uhlelo lokusebenza

I-Stack Rush ngumdlalo we-arcade womdlali oyedwa, olungele yonke iminyaka. Ungadlalwa ngaphandle kokwakha i-akhawunti, ukungena noma ukuhlolwa kweminyaka. Uhlelo aluceli, alunqumi futhi alugcini usuku lokuzalwa lomdlali noma iphrofayela yeminyaka yakhe. Wonke umdlali uthola ukudlala izikhathi 10 ngosuku ngalunye lwe-UTC. Ngemva kokuzisebenzisa, noma yimuphi umdlali angakhetha ngokuzithandela ukubuka isikhangiso somvuzo ukuze agcwalise ukudlala izikhathi 10 lapho lesi sici sitholakala. Ibhodi lezikhundla lansuku zonke eliku-inthanethi liyisici esihlukile; ukubamba iqhaza okusha, ukulanda nokuthumela idatha kusamisiwe kusalindwe ukuhlolwa kokusebenza. Ukuphathwa kwedatha ekhona nokuvulwa kabusha okuchazwe ngezansi kudinga ukukhetha okuhlukile komdlali.

2. Idatha egcinwe kudivayisi yakho

Uhlelo lugcina idatha encane yomdlalo kudivayisi ukuze lukhumbule ukudlala okusele, ukuphakama okugcine ukuqoshwa, ukuphakama okuhle kakhulu, isamba sokuphakama kanye nosuku lwe-UTC olusetshenziswa ukusetha kabusha kwansuku zonke. Alugcini usuku lokuzalwa, iminyaka noma umphumela wephrofayela yeminyaka. Uma umdlali ejoyina ibhodi lezikhundla ngokukhetha okuhlukile, uhlelo lugcina inguqulo yenqubomgomo yebhodi, ukuthi isinqumo senziwe yini, ukukhetha ukujoyina, isikhathi sesinqumo kanye nobuwena obukhishwe yiseva obuhlanganisa imfihlo yomdlali, i-ID yomdlali negama lomdlalo elingaguquki. I-ID noma imfihlo yomdlali yebhodi engaguquki ayidalwa ngaphambi kokukhetha ukujoyina. Izilungiselelo zezikhangiso zendawo ziqopha inguqulo yokukhetha, isinqumo nesikhathi soshintsho ngokwehlukana nemvume ye-Google. Uphawu oludala lokuhlanza ngemva kokuhoxa luhlala ngezicelo zokususa zangaphambili; inani elidala lokwamukela alinakwa ukudlala okuyisisekelo.

Ukulondolozwa kwekhophi ye-Android nokwedluliselwa kudivayisi entsha kuvaliwe kulolu hlelo. Ngakho le datha yokulondoloza ihlala endaweni yohlelo kuleyo divayisi ngaphandle kokuthi ususe uhlelo, usule indawo yalo ngezilungiselelo ze-Android noma isistimu yokusebenza isuse idatha yohlelo.

3. Ibhodi lezikhundla eliku-inthanethi

Ukucubungula okusha kwebhodi lezikhundla kusamisiwe ezinguqulweni zamanje ezilandela ukubuyekezwa. Okulandelayo kuchaza idatha ekhona nokusebenza lapho lesi sici esihlukile singaqala futhi. Izicelo zokususa eziqinisekiswe ngobuwena nezicelo zamalungelo ezikhona zisaqhubeka zitholakala.

Kubadlali abakhetha ngokwehlukile ukujoyina, i-Stack Rush ingathumela amarekhodi ebhodi lansuku zonke endaweni yebhodi esingethwe ku-inthanethi. Ukuthunyelwa kwamaphuzu kuhlanganisa i-ID yomdlali ekhishwe yiseva, ukhiye wosuku lwe-UTC, ukuphakama okuphelele, ukuphakama komzuliswano okuhle kakhulu, uchungechunge oluphezulu, amaphuzu nenguqulo yohlelo. Isevisi isebenzisa usuku lwe-UTC lwayo, isikhathi sokuthumela negama elingaguquki kunokuthemba lawo manani asuka kudivayisi.

Isevisi idala igama elisefomethini engaguquki ethi Rook-XXXXXXXX; lokhu akuyona inkambu yephrofayela yombhalo okhululekile futhi ukuthumela amaphuzu akukwazi ukulishintsha. Izimpendulo zebhodi zomphakathi zibonisa amagama namaphuzu ngokwezikhundla kodwa azivezi i-ID yomdlali eyimfihlo yokuqinisekisa ubuwena. Ukukhetha izikhangiso zomvuzo akukujoyinisi ibhodi lezikhundla. Uhlelo aludali ubuwena bebhodi futhi aluthumeli noma lulande idatha yebhodi ngaphambi kokuthi umdlali akhethe ngokucacile ukujoyina.

Ibhodi lezikhundla likhipha ithokheni yeseshini yesivakashi esayiniwe equkethe i-ID yomdlali, izikhathi zokukhishwa nokuphelelwa yisikhathi nenombolo engahleliwe esetshenziswa kanye. Iphelelwa yisikhathi ngemva kwemizuzu 10, igcinwa kuphela enkumbulweni yenqubo yohlelo futhi ayibhalwa uhlelo endaweni yokugcina yedivayisi noma igcinwe isevisi njengomugqa weseshini.

Uhlelo luthumela imfihlo yomdlali kuphela ukuze luthole iseshini noma lucele ukususa. Isevisi ayigcini leyo mfihlo eluhlaza. Igcina i-hash yobufakazi be-HMAC esebenzisa imfihlo yokusayina, i-ID yomdlali yangaphakathi, igama elingaguquki, isikhathi sokudala nesikhathi seseshini yokugcina.

Ukuvimbela ukuhlukumeza, ibhodi liguqula ikheli le-IP lesicelo libe ukhiye wokukhawulela izicelo we-SHA-256 onosawoti oyimfihlo. Uhlelo lwebhodi alugcini ikheli le-IP eliluhlaza kusizindalwazi salo. I-Cloudflare ingacubungula ngokwehlukile imininingwane yezicelo kumarekhodi ayo esevisi nawokuvikeleka. Imigqa yokukhawulela izicelo iphelelwa yisikhathi ngemva kwehora elilodwa futhi iyasuswa phakathi kwezicelo ezilandelayo nangomsebenzi wokuhlanza wehora ngalinye; ngakho ukususa kungafika ekuhlanzeni okulandelayo ngemva kokuphelelwa yisikhathi.

Amarekhodi amaphuzu ansuku zonke agcinwa kuze kube yizinsuku 30 ze-UTC. Ubufakazi bebhodi obungakatholi iseshini izinsuku 180 buyasuswa kanye namaphuzu abo asele. Umsebenzi wokuhlanza wehora ngalinye wenza lokhu kususa, ngakho kungenzeka ngesikhathi sokuhlanza esilandelayo ngemva kokufinyelelwa komkhawulo wokugcina.

Ngomhla ka-24 ku-Okthoba 2026, siqinisekise ukuthi imisebenzi yokugcina 10 yomsebenzi wokuhlanza ohanjisiwe wehora ngalinye iphumelele. Izibalo eziphelele zazinguziro kumaphuzu, ubufakazi namarekhodi okukhawulela izicelo adlulile emnqamulajuqu wokuhlanzwa, nasemaphuzwini angenabo ubufakazi obuhambisanayo. Uhlelo lwamanje lwe-Workers Free lunikeza iwindi lokubuyisela le-D1 Time Travel lezinsuku eziyisikhombisa. Ama-Logs, Traces ne-Issues ale Worker ayevaliwe, kungekho zindawo ezihlukile zokuthumela idatha ezilungisiwe. Lezi yizilungiselelo nezibalo ezabonwa ngaleso sikhathi, aziwona ubufakazi bokuthi amarekhodi okuvikela omhlinzeki noma umlando wamakhophi asekelayo kusuliwe.

Inqubo yokuphinda kusetshenziswe izicelo zokususa ngemva kokubuyisela ayikaqinisekiswa. Asibuyiseli amakhophi asekelayo omlando enkonzweni ngaphandle uma ukuphinda kusetshenziswe izicelo zokususa nokuhlanza ngokwemikhawulo yokugcina kungaqinisekiswa. Ukucubungula okusha kwebhodi kusamisiwe njengoba lo mbandela wokubuyisela nokuhlolwa kwedatha yezingane nokudluliswa kwamanye amazwe kwebhodi kusalokhu kungaxazululiwe.

4. Ukukhangisa

Ngemva kokusebenzisa ukudlala kwezinsuku zonke okuyizikhathi 10, noma yimuphi umdlali angakhetha ngokuzithandela izikhangiso zomvuzo ze-Google AdMob ukuze agcwalise ukudlala izikhathi 10. Lokhu kunikezwa kuphela ngemva komyalezo wokuphothula isikhangiso somvuzo. Ukukhetha ukungabuki isikhangiso akukujoyinisi ibhodi futhi akudali i-akhawunti yokukhangisa; ukudlala okujwayelekile okuyizikhathi 10 kubuya ekusethweni kabusha kwansuku zonke okulandelayo kwe-UTC.

Ngaphambi kokuqalisa i-SDK yezikhangiso noma ukucela isikhangiso, i-Stack Rush isebenzisa isilungiselelo esisodwa esiphephile ezinganeni kuwo wonke umdlali: izikhangiso zicelwa njengezingaqondene nomuntu futhi eziqondiswe ezinganeni, isicelo simakwa njengesingaphansi kweminyaka yemvume, futhi isilinganiso esiphezulu sokuqukethwe ngu-G. Uma ucela isikhangiso somvuzo ongazikhethela sona okokuqala, uhlelo luxhumana ne-Google ukuze lubuyekeze ukukhetha kobumfihlo kwezikhangiso okusebenzayo, lulinde ukuqedwa kwanoma yiliphi ifomu elidingekayo ngaphambi kwesicelo esifanelekayo. Ungaphinda uvule ukukhetha kobumfihlo okudingekayo ngezilungiselelo zezikhangiso ngaphansi kokungena okuhlala kukhona kokuthi Izilungiselelo. Umhlinzeki we-AdMob oqala ngokuzenzakalela ususiwe esakhiweni se-Android ukuze lezi zilungiselelo zisebenze ngaphambi kokuqalisa.

I-Google nozakwabo bokukhangisa bangacubungula indawo ecishe ibe khona esuselwa ekhelini le-IP, ukusebenzisana nohlelo, ukusebenzisana nezikhangiso, idatha yokuxilonga nezihlonzi zedivayisi noma ezinye izihlonzi ukuze balethe izikhangiso, bakale, bavikele futhi bavimbele ukukhwabanisa ngaphansi kwezinqubomgomo zabo. Imvume ye-ID yokukhangisa ye-Android (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) isusiwe kufayela lezimvume lohlelo, ngakho i-Stack Rush ayiceli ukufinyelela ku-ID Yokukhangisa ye-Android. Ukususa le mvume akuvimbi i-Google ekucubunguleni enye idatha elinganiselwe echazwe ngenhla.

Uhlelo aluthumeli igama, ikheli le-imeyili, usuku lokuzalwa, indawo ye-GPS noma i-ID yebhodi lezikhundla ku-Google ngokukhangisa. Indlela ye-Google yokuphatha izingane ikhawulela izikhangiso eziqondene nomuntu nezokuphinda zimakethwe, izicelo zabahlinzeki bezikhangiso bezinkampani zangaphandle nokuthunyelwa kwe-ID yokukhangisa. Lo mkhawulo obuyekeziwe awuhlanganisi ukulamula okungabuyekeziwe noma ama-SDK ezikhangiso ahlukile, futhi awunikezi indlela yezikhangiso eziqondene nomuntu kubantu abadala.

I-Google inquma isikhathi sokugcina ngokohlobo lwedatha, injongo nezidingo zomthetho noma zokuvikeleka. Inqubomgomo yayo ejwayelekile yokugcina inikeza izibonelo zokususa ingxenye yamakheli e-IP kumarekhodi amaseva okukhangisa ngemva kwezinyanga eziyisishiyagalolunye kanye nolwazi lwamakhukhi ngemva kwezinyanga eziyishumi nesishiyagalombili; amakhophi asekelayo okususa abethelwe angagcina idatha kuze kube yizinyanga eziyisithupha. Lokhu akuwona umnqamulajuqu owodwa wokususa wonke amarekhodi e-AdMob noma isithembiso sokususa ngokushesha esilawulwa uhlelo. Bheka inqubomgomo ye-Google yokugcina nokususa.

5. Idatha uhlelo olungayiqoqi ngamabomu

Ngokusekelwe ekusetshenzisweni kwamanje, i-Stack Rush ayiqoqi noma ithumele ngamabomu imininingwane ye-akhawunti njengegama lakho, ikheli le-imeyili, inombolo yocingo, indawo eqondile, oxhumana nabo, izithombe, okufakwayo kombhobho noma izimfihlo ze-akhawunti yohlelo. Uhlelo aluhlanganisi ukudala i-akhawunti ngaphakathi komdlalo, ingxoxo, ukuthenga ngaphakathi kohlelo noma ukwabelana ngendawo eqondile.

6. Izimvume, ukufinyelela kunethiwekhi nezinsizakalo zepulatifomu

Ifayela lezimvume lohlelo lwe-Android limemezela INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE kanye ne-WAKE_LOCK ngenjini yomdlalo nezici ze-inthanethi ongazikhetha. Imvume ye-ID yokukhangisa ye-Android nezimvume ze-Android Privacy Sandbox ze-ID yezikhangiso, ukulandelela umsuka wezikhangiso nezihloko zisuswa ngokucacile kufayela elihlanganisiwe. Ngakho i-Stack Rush ayiceli ukufinyelela kulezo zihlonzi zokukhangisa noma kuma-API ezihloko zokuthakasela.

Ifayela lezimvume lisusa ngokucacile izimvume ezindala zokugcina okwabelwanayo, isimo socingo, ukuqeda ukuqala kwedivayisi nezinsizakalo ezingaphambili ezilethwa amalabhulali axhumene. I-Stack Rush ayisebenzisi lawo makhono.

Ngokusekelwe ekusetshenzisweni kwamanje, ikhodi yomdlalo ayiceli ukufinyelela kukhamera, umbhobho, oxhumana nabo, ilabhulali yezithombe noma indawo eqondile, futhi ayisebenzisi ama-akhawunti abasebenzisi, ingxoxo noma ukuthenga ngaphakathi kohlelo.

Uma ulanda uhlelo nge-Google Play, i-Google ingacubungula ulwazi lobuchwepheshe noma lokuxilonga ngaphansi kwezinqubomgomo zayo lapho iphatha ukulanda, ukubuyekeza, ukusayina uhlelo, ukubika ukuphahlazeka, ukuhambisana kwedivayisi noma ukusebenza kwesitolo. Lezi zinqubo zepulatifomu zingaphandle kokulawula okuqondile kwe-Stack Rush.

7. Izingane

I-Stack Rush isebenzisa ukuphathwa okufanayo okuphephile ezinganeni kuwo wonke umdlali kunokubuza iminyaka. Uhlelo aluceli, alugcini noma luthumele usuku lokuzalwa noma iphrofayela yeminyaka. Wonke umdlali uthola ukudlala okufanayo kwezikhathi 10 ngosuku futhi angakhetha ngokuzithandela ukugcwalisa ngesikhangiso somvuzo esichazwe ngenhla. Zonke izicelo zezikhangiso zisebenzisa isilungiselelo sokungaqondene nomuntu, esiqondiswe ezinganeni, esingaphansi kweminyaka yemvume nesilinganiso esiphezulu sika-G. Ukujoyina ibhodi kusayisinqumo esicacile esihlukile, futhi abukho ubuwena bebhodi obudalwa ngaphambi kwaleso sinqumo.

Isilinganiso sokuqukethwe sayo yonke iminyaka nezimpawu zezikhangiso eziphephile ezinganeni akuqinisekisi imvume yomzali eqinisekisiwe. Uhlelo alunayo inqubo yemvume yomnakekeli eqinisekisiwe. Ukucubungula okusekelwe emvumeni kwezingane ezingaphansi kweminyaka 14 e-Korea kudinga imvume yommeleli osemthethweni nokuqinisekiswa kwayo. Izikhangiso ze-Android zihlolwa ngaphakathi komkhawulo omncane wokuletha isikhangiso esicelwe ngokuqondile nokuvimbela ukuhlukumeza, kubhekwe izintshisekelo zezingane nezivikelo ngokwehlukile. Lokhu akuhlanganisi ukwakha iphrofayela yokuziphatha noma ukuxhumana ngokumaketha. Imvume edingekayo yokufinyelela kudivayisi okungabalulekile nezivikelo zezingane ezisebenzayo azisuswa uphawu lweminyaka. Ukucubungula idatha entsha yebhodi lezikhundla kusamisiwe ngokwehlukile.

8. Izinguquko kule nqubomgomo

Leli khasi lingabuyekezwa uma ukusebenza kohlelo kushintsha, isibonelo uma izinsizakalo ze-inthanethi, izibalo, izikhangiso noma izici zama-akhawunti zishintsha esikhathini esizayo. Usuku lokuqala ukusebenza phezulu kuleli khasi luzobuyekezwa lapho kushicilelwa izinguquko ezibalulekile.

Ukubuyekezwa kwangomhla ka-24 ku-Okthoba 2026: kwengezwe isifunda se-Cloudflare esibonwe, imiphumela yokuhlanza, izilungiselelo zamarekhodi newindi lokubuyisela lezinsuku eziyisikhombisa; kwacaciswa ukuthi amakhophi asekelayo omlando akufanele abuyiselwe enkonzweni ngaphandle kokuphinda kusetshenziswe ukususa okuqinisekisiwe. Lokhu kubuyekezwa akungezi ukuqoqwa okusha kwedatha futhi akuqali kabusha ukucubungula kwebhodi.

9. Izicelo zokususa idatha

Ukuze ucele ukususa idatha yebhodi lezikhundla eliku-inthanethi le-Stack Rush, thumela i-imeyili ku-support@mibstudio.top ufake igama lohlelo negama lakho lebhodi elithi Rook-XXXXXXXX. Umshicileli angasebenzisa indlela yomlawuli evikelekile yokususa ukuze asuse ubufakazi obuhambisanayo nawo wonke amarekhodi abo ebhodi ngaphandle kokukucela ukuthi uveze i-ID yomdlali eyimfihlo noma imfihlo yakhe.

Ezinguqulweni ezilandela ukuhlolwa kokusebenza, sebenzisa ID · Phatha esikrinini somphumela womdlalo ukuze uhoxe. Ukuthunyelwa kwamaphuzu kuma kuqala. Isicelo sokususa sithunyelwa kusetshenziswa ubufakazi obukhona. Kuze kube impendulo ye-HTTP 200 iqinisekisa ukususa, ubufakazi obuncane bugcinelwa ukuzama futhi ekuqaliseni okulandelayo noma esenzweni sokuphatha, futhi ukubhalisa kabusha kuyavinjwa. Uhlelo lubonisa Zama ukususa futhi ngesikhathi kusalindwe. Amarekhodi namakhophi asekelayo omhlinzeki kwehlukile. Izinguqulo ezindala zingasula ubufakazi bendawo noma isicelo seseva sihluleka; ukusula indawo yokugcina noma ukukhipha uhlelo akusikho ukususa kuseva.

Igama lakho elisesidlangalaleni lodwa alibufakazeli ubunikazi. Umshicileli angadinga ukuqinisekisa okwengeziwe okuncane futhi uzokwazisa ngokuqeda noma ngokungasuswa ngenxa yomthetho wokugcina. Ungathumeli imfihlo yomdlali wakho. Wena noma umnakekeli ningacela ukufinyelela, ukulungisa, ukukhawulela noma ukuhoxa ngale ndlela yokuxhumana efanayo.

Idatha yomdlalo yendawo egcinwe kudivayisi yakho ingasuswa ngokukhipha uhlelo noma ngokusula indawo yalo kuzilungiselelo ze-Android. Idatha yezikhangiso ephethwe yi-Google AdMob noma yi-Google Play ilawulwa ngezilawuli ze-Google zobumfihlo nezikhangiso.
